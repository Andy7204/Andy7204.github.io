Privacy Policy

Todo for Android · Last updated 21 October 2026

Todo is a to-do list app that works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

What the app stores

The tasks and lists you create (titles, deadlines, reminder and repeat settings, priority) and your app settings. They are stored only on your device.

What the app collects or shares

Nothing. Todo does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or usage data with the developer or with third parties. The app does not request the internet permission.

Backups

  • Android backup: if you have turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. You can turn this off in your phone's settings.
  • Export backup: when you choose “Export backup”, the app writes a file to a location you pick. What happens to that file is up to you.

Permissions

  • Notifications – to show reminders and the optional daily summary.
  • Alarms & reminders – so reminders arrive exactly on time.
  • Run at startup – to re-schedule reminders after your phone restarts.

Deleting your data

Delete tasks in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
